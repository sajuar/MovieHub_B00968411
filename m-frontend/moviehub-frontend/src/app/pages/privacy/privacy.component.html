Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account on MovieHub we collect your username, email address and a hashed password. We never store plain-text passwords. When you write a review we store your rating, comment and the date it was submitted.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to operate the MovieHub platform — authenticating your account, displaying your reviews and managing your watchlist. We do not sell, share or rent your personal data to third parties.

3. Cookies & Session Tokens

MovieHub uses short-lived session cookies to keep you logged in. These cookies expire automatically after a period of inactivity and are never used for tracking or advertising purposes.

4. Data Security

All passwords are hashed using bcrypt before being stored in the database. Session tokens have a time-limited expiry and are invalidated on logout. Login attempts are rate-limited to protect against brute-force attacks.

5. Your Rights

You may delete your account at any time by contacting us. Upon deletion, all your reviews and personal data will be permanently removed from the database.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or how your data is handled, please use the Contact Us page.

MovieHub is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy is reviewed periodically and may be updated to reflect changes in our practices. Continued use of the platform after any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
